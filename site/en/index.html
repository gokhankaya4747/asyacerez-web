About Asya Çerez

Carrying the richness of Anatolia to the world — reliably

Asya Çerez Foreign Trade Ltd. is a nuts and dried fruits trading company based in Fatih, Istanbul's historic trading quarter. We bring Türkiye's world-famous hazelnuts, pistachios, apricots, figs and raisins to international markets, while importing cashews, almonds, walnuts and dates from the world's leading growing regions.

  • Import, export and wholesale supply under one roof
  • Sold by the tonne — palletised, LCL or full container
  • Testing and traceability on every lot

Bulk Packing & Loading

Export-grade packing, secure loading

We sell wholesale only, in vacuum cartons, bags and big bags. Palletising, container stowage and loading are planned to protect freshness and safety, and carton labels follow destination-country regulations.

  • 10 kg vacuum-packed carton
  • 12.5 / 25 kg carton with PE liner
  • 25 / 50 kg PP or jute bag
  • Big bag (500 – 1,000 kg) · palletised and container loading
