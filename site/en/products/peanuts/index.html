Peanuts

Arachis hypogaea

Osmaniye, the heart of Turkish peanut production, is known for large kernels, thin skins and a distinctive aroma. We offer domestic and imported peanuts in forms from in-shell snacking nuts to raw kernels for peanut butter.

OriginTürkiye +
GTİP / HS1202.41
Shelf life12 months

Wholesale only · samples and lab reports available on request.

OriginTürkiye (Osmaniye, Adana), Argentina, USA
FormsIn-shell, kernels, blanched, roasted, salted
Grades / Sizes38/42 · 40/50 · 50/60 · 60/70 count per ounce
Moisture≤ 7%
Shelf life12 months under proper storage
HS Code1202.41 (in-shell) · 1202.42 (shelled) · 2008.11 (roasted)
Sales termsWholesale only · pallet, LCL or container (20’ / 40’)

Typical specification values; binding values are stated in the contract and lot analysis report.

  • In-shell roasted (salted / unsalted), raw kernels, blanched and roasted kernels.
  • Runner, Virginia and domestic NC-7 types.
  • Industrial grades for peanut butter and confectionery.
  • Aflatoxin testing mandatory for every lot.

Approximate nutrition per 100 g (raw product). Values vary by variety and lot.

Energy567 kcal

Packaging options

  • 10 kg vacuum-packed carton
  • 12.5 / 25 kg carton with PE liner
  • 25 / 50 kg PP or jute bag
  • Big bag (500 – 1,000 kg) · palletised and container loading

Storage conditions

  • Store cool (0–10 °C), dry (RH < 65%) and away from light.
  • Keep on pallets, off the floor and away from strong odours.
  • Reseal opened packs airtight.
  • Cold storage recommended for long-term holding.
