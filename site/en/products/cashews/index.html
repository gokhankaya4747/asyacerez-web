Cashews

Anacardium occidentale

With its creamy texture and mildly sweet taste, cashew demand keeps growing — from snack mixes to plant-based foods. We source Vietnamese, Indian and West African cashews graded to international AFI standards.

Grades / SizesW180 · W210 · W240 · W320 · W450 · S · B · LWP

Sales termsWholesale only · pallet, LCL or container (20’ / 40’)

Typical specification values; binding values are stated in the contract and lot analysis report.

  • Whole white kernels: W180 (largest), W210, W240, W320, W450.
  • Broken grades: Splits (S), Butts (B), Large White Pieces (LWP) — cost-effective for industry.
  • Raw, roasted, salted and flavoured roasted options.
  • Vacuum / nitrogen-flushed tins and cartons keep kernels fresh.

Packaging options

  • 10 kg vacuum-packed carton
  • 12.5 / 25 kg carton with PE liner
  • 25 / 50 kg PP or jute bag
  • Big bag (500 – 1,000 kg) · palletised and container loading

Storage conditions

  • Store cool (0–10 °C), dry (RH < 65%) and away from light.
  • Keep on pallets, off the floor and away from strong odours.
  • Reseal opened packs airtight.
  • Cold storage recommended for long-term holding.
